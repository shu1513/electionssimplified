## Source, current code and donor types

Rhode Island publishes candidate independent expenditures and other covered disbursements through CF-8 filings, indexed by ERTS and available as scanned PDFs. The current [2026 CF-8 guide and campaign manual](https://elections.ri.gov/campaign-finance/manuals) distinguish independent expenditures, electioneering communications and covered transfers. A legal reporting requirement does not establish candidate allocation. Qualify current amendment lineage, the source-reported transaction basis, refunds and multi-target allocation before accepting outside money. The currently linked CF-8 instructions define an independent expenditure or electioneering communication as made on first public dissemination; a covered transfer is made when funds are transferred or paid. Do not relabel reported communications as cash paid or require a nonexistent cash-payment field.

The current module has a WebForms client, artifact acquisition/cache, report/version parsers, a CF-2 selector, receiving aggregator, resolver/auto-link, writer wrapper and ballot loader. It exposes probe/raw-refresh commands. The consolidation plan deliberately describes Rhode Island as import-style; absence of a batch runner alone is not a defect. Reuse existing contracts and add the smallest scoped import/sync path that fits. Outside source acquisition must remain independent of receiving finance.

Native receiving exports explicitly distinguish PAC and Party cash from their aggregate, in-kind and loan categories. Recognize typed itemized cash without generic-name inference, distinguish unnamed aggregates from named donors, and qualify signed returns before claiming net positions. Source records and uncapped proof remain private.

The new component's basis is gross positive itemized native PAC/Party cash receipts, before separately reported expenditure-side returns. State that basis explicitly; it is not a net position or a claim to identify every registered donor. Unknown types/summary return labels, nonpositive committee receipts, duplicate native IDs, incomplete certified periods and unreconciled exports hold the whole component. A native negative `Refund of Contribution` with `TransType=Expenditure` and zero public match is separately reported expenditure-side money: retain it for complete export/summary closure and exclude it from gross receipts. Wrong-direction, positive, zero, public-matched or summary-only return variants remain held. Preserve equal gifts as separate native occurrences. Require every exported type to match the native period summary and the PAC/Party cash lines to close against the current CF-2, including separately counted unnamed aggregates. Summary-only groups not reproduced by the export remain a qualification gap for this component.

## Reporting deadlines and manual reruns

The post-election report is due **December 1, 2026**, covering October 27–November 30. Q4 is due **February 1, 2027**, covering December 1–31. Use actual certified periods and the updated calendar rather than older primary-date flyers.

The [currently linked official CF-8 instructions](https://elections.ri.gov/sites/g/files/xkgbur756/files/2024-03/CF-8%20121923%20Fillable.pdf) require an initial report within seven days after calendar-year covered disbursements exceed $1,000; an initial report required within 30 days of an election is due within 24 hours. Subsequent reports are triggered by additional covered disbursements of at least $1,000 for the same election and generally due within seven days. The subsequent-report paragraph repeats an initial-report near-election exception: preserve that source wording and check current Board guidance rather than inferring a broader deadline. Report periods must be consecutive. The ordinary pre-election schedule alone does not establish CF-8 completeness.
