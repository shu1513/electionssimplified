# Massachusetts finance gaps

## Sources and existing behavior

OCPF publishes separate IEPAC and ordinary independent-expenditure reports. The current client reads the IEPAC inventory and report details, but the sync fetches receipts, IE and YTD data in one `Promise.all`. A failed receipt/YTD request prevents IE processing; individual IE detail failures are skipped and a report limit can truncate the inventory. The batch also uses legacy summary clocks. IE acquisition needs its own source qualification, due selection and failure preservation before the legacy leg.

The [public API guide](https://api.ocpf.us/developers/guide) documents anonymous report, filer and item APIs. The official reports application also exposes ordinary IE inventory, controls and detail routes. The flat ordinary expense feed is not a complete financial ledger: the observed 502 rows did not reconcile to the current 44-report controls. Work from current filed reports and their amendment families instead. The downloads page distinguishes the independent-only export from general filer-report data; the independent-only export excludes IEPACs, rather than every raw dataset excluding them.

Native item type 202 is Committee Contribution and 402 is Committee Inkind Contribution. Individual, union/association, non-contribution, interest, loan, savings-transfer and anonymous aggregate types are distinct. The actual item payload exposes `contributorCpfId` and `recordTypeId`; it does not populate the existing DTO's `contributorType`. Explicit committee receipts support reported named groups without promoting organizations by name. Preserve reported labels and distinguish them from verified registered identities. Existing direct storage/readers expose occupation and receipt-size breakdowns, not these named committee groups.

## Deadlines and required reruns

The official [filer schedule](https://www.ocpf.us/Filers/FilerTypes) and captured 2026 schedule put legislative pre-election deposit/supplemental reports on **October 26**, through October 16. Legislative late contributions run October 17–30; qualifying deposited contributions of $500 or more require reporting within 72 hours. Monthly bank reports are due the fifth. Legislative year-end reports are due **January 20, 2027**. Select other candidate/party/PAC schedules by actual filer type.

The official [IEPAC form](https://files.ocpf.us/pdf/forms/cpf_m102_iepac.pdf) and [ordinary IE form](https://files.ocpf.us/pdf/forms/cpf_m18a_2018.pdf) require reports after the stated $250 aggregate threshold, normally within seven business days. During the final reporting window they require a preliminary report within 24 hours; an initially unknown value may be supplied later. Do not interpret a preliminary missing value as zero. No single October 26 report closes IE disclosure.

Rerun **October 27/28**, after the pre-election deadline; **November 4/6**, after rapid reports and October bank activity; and **January 21/23, 2027**, after legislative year-end. Check later amendments and retain the post-election cohort through these deadlines. These are operational instructions, not new automations.
