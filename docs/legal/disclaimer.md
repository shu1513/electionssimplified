Elections Simplified content is not official election information. Official election information must be verified directly with the relevant government election authority.

## 5. Address lookup and district matching

Address lookup, geocoding, district matching, ballot matching, and jurisdiction matching are provided only as estimates to help identify elections and civic information that may be relevant to an address.

These features may be affected by geocoding errors, boundary changes, redistricting, missing data, typographical errors, apartment or unit issues, data-source delays, and differences between residential address, mailing address, registration address, and precinct records.

A matched address, district, precinct, jurisdiction, polling place, ballot, or election shown on Elections Simplified is not an official determination of where you live, where you are registered, where you are eligible to vote, where you must vote, what ballot you will receive, or what districts apply to you.

These summaries are for general educational purposes only. They may omit details, simplify complex legal language, reflect interpretation, or fail to capture all legal, fiscal, practical, or local consequences.

Before voting or taking a position on a ballot measure, read the full official ballot text, official voter guide, fiscal analysis, legal analysis, and any official materials from the relevant election authority.
